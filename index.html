Money terms in plain words
PAC
A political action committee: a group that collects donations and gives money directly to candidates' campaigns, within legal limits. FEC: PACs
Super PAC
A committee that may raise and spend unlimited amounts to support or oppose candidates, but may not give to a campaign or coordinate with it. FEC: spending data
Independent expenditure
Money spent on ads, mail or other messages that urge a vote for or against a candidate, made without the candidate's campaign. FEC: independent expenditures
Earmarked contribution
A donation a person asks a PAC to pass on to a named candidate; the PAC only forwards it, and it is counted in the PAC column here. FEC: what PACs may pay
